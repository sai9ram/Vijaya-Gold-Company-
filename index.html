Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my gold valued?

expand_more

We use modern German machines to check your gold's purity safely, without melting it. We then calculate the price using today's live gold rate. This ensures you get the fairest and best price possible.

What documents are required when we buy gold?

expand_more

For a safe transaction that follows government rules, please bring a valid ID (like Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Driving License) and address proof. If we are releasing your gold from a bank, we also need those bank receipts.

Do you buy pledged gold from banks?

expand_more

Yes! If you have taken a gold loan, we can help. We pay off your loan amount to the bank to release your gold. Then, we buy it from you at today's best market price and pay you the remaining cash.

How long does the entire process take?

expand_more

The whole process takes around 15 to 30 minutes. This includes testing the gold, weighing it, and calculating the final price. Once you agree, we transfer your money instantly.

Do you buy broken or damaged gold jewelry?

expand_more

Absolutely! We buy gold in any condition—whether it's broken, damaged, single earrings, or even scrap gold. We value it strictly based on its weight and purity, regardless of its physical appearance.

Is there any fee for testing the purity of my gold?

expand_more

No, we offer 100% free gold purity testing using our world-class German XRF machines. There are absolutely no hidden charges, even if you choose not to proceed after the valuation.

What payment methods are available?

expand_more

We provide multiple instant payment options for your convenience. You can choose to receive your money via Cash (within statutory limits), Instant Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS), or UPI (GPay, PhonePe, etc.) immediately after the transaction.

Do you buy silver articles and jewelry as well?

expand_more

Yes, we also buy all kinds of silver articles, including silver coins, bars, jewelry, and utensils. We offer the most competitive market rates for silver in Tirupati with the same transparent process as gold.
