  • check_circle Identity Information: Full name, date of birth, and government-issued ID details (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License) as required by law for gold transactions.
  • check_circle Contact Information: Phone number, email address, and residential address.
  • check_circle Transaction Information: Details of gold or silver items you bring to us, weight, purity test results, valuation amounts, and payment details.
  • check_circle Website Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and cookies for improving our website experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • check_circle To process your gold and silver buying transactions accurately and fairly.
  • check_circle To verify your identity as required by Indian government regulations for precious metal transactions.
  • check_circle To contact you regarding your inquiries, quotes, or transaction updates.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following cases:
